== 1. what was there (79a6703f) ==
# The ticket's four, each `error: … the call of `….commanded_speed/2` [`….speed_max/2`] can be answered by 2
# clauses of the predicate …, and a proof reads a call by ONE` — KF2SD's refusal, as filed.
#
# A RECURSIVE PREDICATE OF ONE CLAUSE ABORTS THE BINARY. The emitter had no guard against reading a clause of a
# predicate inside a clause of the same predicate: one clause is read without a choice, and read again, until the

#   same in two predicates, has none of its eleven). Of several clauses KF2SD refused before it got there, which is
