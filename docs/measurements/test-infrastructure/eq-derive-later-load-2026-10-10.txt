# The classification — every composite, its boundary, its fields, the fixpoint — is 1 ms in either load.
# The rest is reads of the provider relation, made with `kb.provides_index` at `None`, where `sort_provides` and
# the conditions lookup each fall back to a scan of the whole relation.
# The later load is the DEARER of the two, deriving nothing: the relation it scans now holds the rows the
# stdlib's load derived, and the 60 candidates are the stdlib's composites that load found underivable — asked
# again, field by field, at every load, and found underivable again (77 candidates - 17 derived = 60).
