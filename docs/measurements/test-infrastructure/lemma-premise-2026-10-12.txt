== 2. the decisions (user, 2026-10-12) ==
#   ON SECTION 1 — what a cited lemma's premise becomes in its lift when it is a rule's predicate, a bodied
#   rule's, a fact's, a one-argument rule's alike. Offered: (a) the lemma is REFUSED as not citable while it has
#   one; (b) the predicate is an UNINTERPRETED relation, the lift's premise and the consumer's own call both
#   written with it; (c) the callee's body is read into the antecedent.
#   DECIDED: (b). The predicate is a declared `Bool` function; a consumer states its own call of a relation a
#   cite declared — the call it skips and the call it reads the body of — and only those, so a proof that cites
#   no such lemma has the document it had; a call that cannot be written is refused. Premises only: a conclusion
#   that is a rule's predicate stays "not citable".
#   NOT CHOSEN, and why: (a) leaves a structured proof's step unable to state a premise that is one of its
#   parent's rule calls, and lf1's two rules citable only `by trust`; (c) is held by no consumer that skips its
#   call, and drags into the lift the facts and the nonlinearity the lift exists to keep out.
#   A LAW's such premise is as WI-20261012-ZZ7PH left it: the law is not lowered, and said.
#   TAKEN AS FOLLOWING, and said to the user with the result: what cannot be written whole is REFUSED and not
#   left out, beyond the shapes the question named — a premise that is a connective over goals, an entity
#   premise's slot that is no variable, a call inside an inlined rule that names the rule's own variable
#   (section 6). A connective written as the relation of its name is `(anthill.kernel.or/2 (>= var_0 1.0) (>=
#   var_0 2.0))`, uninterpreted — sound, discharged by no consumer, and nothing said (measured on the first cut:
#   the citing proof was a COUNTEREXAMPLE with no word of why).
#   AFTER THE REVIEW (section 6, finding 8): RECORDED WITNESSES ARE RECORDED AGAIN — `WITNESS_FORMAT_VERSION`
#   3 → 4. A proof that cited such a lemma has a sidecar that says `Discharged`, its state hash unmoved, and a
#   sidecar vouches for a cite. Offered against leaving it, as ZZ7PH did for its own fixes; decided: a soundness
#   fix of the emitter moves the witness version.
#   AND WHAT WAS FOUND BESIDE IT IS FILED (section 7), each by the user's word: four tickets.
