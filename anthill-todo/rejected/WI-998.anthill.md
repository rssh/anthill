## Description

Implements 059 R4 clauses 1 and 2 — FILL, never DISPLACE — on top of the pass-1
declaration ledger. Spec: docs/proposals/059-secondary-entries.md.

CLAUSE 1 — TWO SUPPLIERS OF ONE MEMBER NAME ARE REFUSED AT LOAD, naming both sites.
058 §3.1's coexistence-gated-on-nameability: two providers of a SPEC may coexist
because an ambiguous call has a repair the author can write (`[Spec = W]`); two
members of one sort under one name have no such currency — `r.peek()` cannot say
which — so this is the case 058 calls "coexistence one cannot select out of is a
trap, not a feature". Applies to two secondary entries, and to two same-named
operations in one main entry.

CLAUSE 2 — A SECONDARY ENTRY MAY NOT REDECLARE A MEMBER THE MAIN ENTRY DECLARES AND
IMPLEMENTS. 058 §3.6's no-displacement, in its own words: fill silence, never
overwrite speech.

"IMPLEMENTS" IS NOT "HAS A BODY", AND GETTING THIS WRONG SILENTLY DISCARDS THE FILL.
MEASURED: `sort String`'s `isEmpty` is declared body-less in stdlib and mapped to
`string_is_empty` by an `operation_map` in anthill-stl. A secondary entry supplying
`operation isEmpty(s: String) -> Bool = false` — wrong for every input — LOADS CLEAN
AND NEVER RUNS: `"".isEmpty()` still answers true and `"x".isEmpty()` still false,
identical to the control with no secondary entry at all. The author is told nothing.
The mechanism: `call_op_sym` and `call_op_bridged` BOTH return a registered builtin
before any body lookup, and an `operation_map` clause arrives there AS a registered
builtin (`register_operation_mappings` -> `register_builtin_sym`).

SO SILENCE MEANS NO IMPLEMENTATION FROM ANY SOURCE — no anthill body, no
`operation_map`/`const_map` entry for the target language, no registered builtin —
and a fill over a declaration backed by any of them is REFUSED, naming the backing.

NOT A CORNER CASE: counting `operation_map` entries in anthill-stl, Float has 29,
String 13, Int64 9, BigInt 7, persistence 6 = 64 members that read as "silence" under
a body-only test.

`[simp]` IS A FOURTH CASE AND A DIFFERENT ONE — name it, do not fold it in. A `[simp]`
equation does not BACK the operation (WI-818/885), but the typer rewrites LHS->RHS
before dispatch, so a filled body is dead at every rewritten call site and live
everywhere else.

WHERE THE CHECK RUNS. Clause 1 is a pure ledger walk in pass 1. Clause 2 SPLITS: the
ledger decides "redeclared", but "already implemented" can only be read after the
`anthill.realization.OperationMapping` facts exist — and the declaration and its
mapping routinely live in different crates (String's declaration in stdlib, its
mapping in anthill-stl). So clause 2 needs a post-pass-2 read as well.

WHAT THIS CLAUSE IS FOR, and why the body-less carve-out must survive: it carries
SPEC-AUTHOR AND IMPLEMENTER AS DIFFERENT HANDS — one writes the sort and its
signatures, another (often an AI agent) supplies the bodies in anthill, and the
interface it must meet is checkable rather than conventional. The signature stays the
main entry's and the filling entry may not change it.

MEASURED BLAST RADIUS: 0 sites for both clauses. Honest but easy — the corpus
contains no secondary entries at all, so clause 2 has nothing to bite on yet.
CONTROL: a fill over a body-less-AND-unimplemented member must still LOAD (that is the
capability); a fill over `String.isEmpty` must newly FAIL, and today passes.

## Reason

Superseded by WI-1049 and the 2026-08-08 proposal 059 decision. WI-1049 already refuses every second operation declaration; proposal 059 now refuses bodyless operations in secondary entries instead of allowing another declaration to fill them. Implementation belongs to WI-1000.
