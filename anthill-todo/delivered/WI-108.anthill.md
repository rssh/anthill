## Description

Phase 2.6b — nested-implication / forall resolution in the SLD evaluator. Use-time consumer of WI-105/106/107: when a rule body contains `(forall(?x), Q(?x) -: P(?x))` and the goal is P(?t), the resolver needs to discharge Q(?t) under the substitution ?x↦?t (introducing a fresh witness for the bound variable) rather than treat the implication opaquely. Pattern-fragment unification (Miller) for the predicate-variable case. Deliverables: kb/resolve.rs handles NestedImpl goals; eager universal instantiation when ?P is unified by the outer caller. Acceptance: a query against List.induction (WI-106) succeeds for a concretely-instantiated ?P; lf1 reachability proof terminates with `unsat` for both base and step subgoals. Required by WI-101 if the induction tactic dispatches sub-queries through the SLD evaluator rather than purely via SMT.
