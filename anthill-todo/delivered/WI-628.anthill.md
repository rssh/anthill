## Description

Resolver: silent depth-limit truncation lets NAF and definite-failure consumers DECIDE from an incomplete search. step_init pops a frame at max_depth (resolve.rs ~591) indistinguishably from definite failure; step_naf then reads an exhausted-but-truncated inner stream as 'no proof -> not(P) SUCCEEDS' — with default max_depth=100, not(P) over any derivation deeper than the REMAINING budget (step_naf passes max_depth - depth, resolve.rs ~1709) wrongly succeeds; same for any caller treating resolve()==[] as refutation. Violates the never-NAF-decide-undecided discipline (WI-067/WI-519) for truncated searches. WI-616 added the substrate: SearchStream.truncated (set at the depth pop) and the semantic-eq dispatch already answers UNDECIDED when its sub-resolution truncated — but NAF and other empty-result consumers do not consult it. SCOPE: thread truncated into step_naf's three-way (truncated+empty => flounder, not success), decide which other resolve() consumers must check it (prove/constraint guards under definite_only, forall/bounded-quant discharge, typer prove_from_gamma), and consider surfacing truncation on Solution/ResolveStats for hosts. Found during WI-616 /code-review (finder D).
