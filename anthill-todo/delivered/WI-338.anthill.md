## Description

Code-review WI-326 F8/F11/F9: pair_present_labels / cover_present_labels hardening. THREE small issues bundled because they touch the same family of helpers: (F8) functor-name pre-filter at typing.rs ~5395 (in pair_present_labels) and ~5705 (cover_present_labels) skips cross-arm pairings whose functors differ. Pre-WI-326 arrow_compatible called types_compatible(ae, ee) directly, which dispatches the (sort_ref, parameterized) and (parameterized, sort_ref) arms (typing.rs ~5615-5620) treating bare sort vs parameterized as compatible. pair/cover_present_labels never attempts unify_types in that case; drops the label into only_a. Fix: remove the strict functor-name pre-filter and let unify_types decide (small perf hit, less surprising semantics) OR add explicit (sort_ref, parameterized) bridge arms. (F11) pair_present_labels has no rollback (acknowledged in docstring) — partial-bind contamination when unify_types binds a Var then fails. Local-subst use in arrow_compatible isolates the impact but the helper is generally callable with a non-scratch subst. Fix: snapshot/restore subst around each unify_types attempt OR call a probe-only variant. (F9) Both-open arm in subtype_effect_rows (and unify_effect_rows) allocates kb.fresh_var(?rho) + kb.alloc(Term::Var) on every invocation — local_subst is discarded but kb.next_var grows monotonically and the term store accumulates orphan Var nodes. Fix: cache a thread-local Symbol for '?rho' OR memoize the result of subtype_effect_rows on (actual_effects, expected_effects) so repeat queries don't re-allocate. ACCEPTANCE: cross-arm sort_ref-vs-parameterized labels pair correctly; subst rollback semantics documented or implemented; kb.next_var growth bounded under stress. Full cargo-test green.
