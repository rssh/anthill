### 2026-05-21T12:30:11Z — feedback — user

Prerequisite from WI-277: the simp_rewrite engine's walk (rewrite / map_children / substitute_to_occurrence in kb/simp_rewrite.rs) is recursive over occurrence-tree depth. Before WI-278 ships [simp]/dot rules that fire on real (possibly deeply-nested) operation bodies, convert the walk to an explicit work-stack — mirroring NodeOccurrence::Drop and the typing pass, which were made iterative to survive the 624-line typing_pass_spec.anthill. Until then the engine is inert (run() early-returns when no [simp] rule is indexed), so it cannot overflow. Documented as a v1 limitation in the simp_rewrite.rs module doc.
