## Description

RETIRE ModifyRuntime AND THE GENERIC Modify HANDLER: step 1 of docs/design/037-implementation.md. Re-scoped by the user on 2026-10-17 (the plan's decision D1). Revised proposal 037 has no generic runtime spec: a base sort's runtime is its own operations, and a sort with no operations of its own has nothing that writes it (037 §3.5, §4.1). So this ticket's original goal, keeping box(id: 1) and box(id: 2) apart in the generic handler's functor-keyed map, has no counterpart; its notes below are the history that led here. WHAT GOES (plan §2.1): stdlib sort ModifyRuntime and the header paragraphs about it (prelude/effects.anthill); Cell's provides ModifyRuntime[T = Self, V = V] (prelude/cell.anthill); the binding block in rustland/anthill-stl/anthill/effects.anthill; the HOST_FNS rows and effect dispatchers in eval/builtins.rs; default_modify_handler with resource_key, detect_cycle and detect_cycle_term, and its registration (eval/effects.rs); the EvalError variants only it produces (CyclicReference, ResourceWithFields, ResourceUnset); tests/common's register_modify_handler. THE TESTS move as plan §2.1 lists them. The handler's own tests go, replaced by one row: the m5 shape refused at load. The tests that used it as a fixture keep their subject with another: the registry rows on Time.Clock; 0RP29's re-key pair on a spec and override of its own; 4GBQV's typing rows on a fixture operation that carries Modify[target]; BRT4Y's MIGRATED list, 46 to 44; scaland BootstrapTest's emitted list. The installers that never reach the handler lose the call. The comments that cite the withdrawn 037 are fixed with it (plan §2.11). kernel-language §5.3's ModifyRuntime example (line 2127) goes once its wording is agreed (plan D4). Acceptance: plan §4, step 1.
