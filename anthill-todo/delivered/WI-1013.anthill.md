TermView DROPS an Expr::Apply's type_args, so a GoalKey is not injective and THREE consumers can silently collapse two distinct goals. occ_head's Expr::Apply arm (term_view.rs ~863) destructures { functor, pos_args, named_args, .. } -- the `..` is type_args -- and they never reach pos_arg / named_arg / named_keys. So f[T = Int64](x) and f[T = String](x) present IDENTICAL views. THREE CONSUMERS INHERIT IT, each with a different failure mode: (1) goal_fingerprint/GoalKey -- the resolver's seen_goals answer-dedup (WI-348) DROPS the second of two answers differing only in a bracket, and query_cache, keyed on the same GoalKey, can serve one goal's candidate list for another; (2) GoalKey::is_opaque_free, the fact-dedup injectivity guard WI-815 introduced, answers TRUE on such a key, so value_fact_dedup_key would OVER-dedup -- and over-dedup DROPS A FACT, not merely precision; (3) views_structurally_equal reports two bracket-distinct occurrences EQUAL, which is what WI-762's receiver-divergence guard reads. UNREACHABLE TODAY and known to be: WI-472 recorded it as "no producer mints a type_args-bearing Apply inside a value-fact head", and WI-815 re-states it at value_fact_dedup_key as the one hole its guard cannot see -- which is why that predicate is named is_opaque_free (what it CHECKS) rather than is_injective (what it is FOR). WHAT MAKES IT REACHABLE is WI-839, whose whole purpose is to close every silent drop of the call-site bracket channel at all three producers; once brackets are populated, a type_args-bearing Apply at a goal or head position stops being hypothetical. NOT a duplicate of WI-322 (Delivered), which fixed collect_unbound_vars_node's VAR walk over these same fields -- the var walkers already chase type_args via collect_expr_termid_field_vars; it is the VIEW that does not, so this is the same gap one layer over. MEASURE THE DISCRIM BLAST RADIUS BEFORE LANDING: insert_walk_args walks pos_arity then named_keys, so adding children changes stored-pattern keying for every Apply-headed clause and every query -- take candidate-set counts over the full corpus before and after, the instrument wi814_lambda_view_test already uses for exactly this.
