## Description

Implement proposal 059 R3: classify the direct content of every secondary entry with default-deny semantics. A secondary entry may add members and spec claims, never identity.

OPERATION RULE: an operation declared in a secondary entry must be fresh and must have a runnable Anthill body. Refuse a bodyless declaration even if a host mapping could later back it. A `[simp]` equation is not an operation body. Duplicate declarations across main and secondary entries are already refused by WI-1049 and are a control for this ticket, not another implementation path. Ordinary namespaces and bodyless operations in a main entry remain legal.

ALLOWED: fresh body-having operations and operation-block sugar; consts; nested sort or enum declarations with bodies, whose own addresses make them main entries; same-entry proofs; `provides` declarations and host realization blocks. The interior of a host `provides` block is recursively classified.

REFUSED: entities; type-parameter declarations; sort-level requires; every rule until WI-1001 can enforce the narrow fresh-head rule; every fact and constraint except the explicit `provides` spelling; foreign-target proofs; and any unclassified production.

CLASSIFY BEFORE IMPLEMENTATION: bodyless type aliases, standalone type-parameter binders, and `describe` declarations. Imports are governed separately by WI-995.

ACCEPTANCE: drive a fresh body-having secondary operation through dispatch and assert its value. Refuse a fresh bodyless secondary operation with a diagnostic naming the sort and declaration. Pin controls showing that a main-entry bodyless host-facing operation remains legal, a duplicate declaration is refused by WI-1049, and ordinary namespaces retain their existing content rules. Exercise the same operation rule through operation-block sugar. Run rustland/scripts/test.sh.
