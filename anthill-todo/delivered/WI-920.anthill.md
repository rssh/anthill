## Description

ONE TOOL'S DOMAIN IS BAKED INTO THE CORE WRITE SEAM. `KnowledgeBase::assert_persistent` and `persist_mirrored` (rustland/anthill-core/src/kb/extent.rs ~534 and ~553) both stamp every resident persisted fact with `let sort = self.intern("Fact"); let domain = self.intern("anthill.todo");` — a literal, unconditional pair. So a row written through proposal 057's general write seam by ANY embedder lands in anthill-todo's domain, and its sort is the kernel `Fact` regardless of what the row's own head declares. Note the contrast INSIDE THE SAME FILE: `assert_checked_persistent` (~435) derives the sort from the row (`self.fact_trigger_sort(&row).or(sort_hint)`) and refuses loud when it can find neither — "row has no trigger sort and no sort hint" — so the seam already knows how to ask; two of its three writers just do not. `update_persistent`'s resident arm is the third behaviour again: it INHERITS `rule_sort`/`rule_domain` from the row it replaces, which is right and is also why the discrepancy is invisible in tests (a fact asserted with the hardcoded pair keeps it across updates, self-consistently). WHAT IS AT STAKE is not cosmetic: `domain` is what the loader and the reflect queries partition a KB by, so a mis-domained fact is one a domain-scoped query does not see. NOT DRIVEN — code-read, noticed while delivering WI-916 (which changed the mounted arm of `assert_persistent` and left the resident arm untouched). FIRST STEP: establish who actually reaches the resident arm today (the mounted arm returns before it; `persist_mirrored` is the mirror path) and whether any non-anthill-todo caller does, before deciding whether this is live or latent. ACCEPTANCE: the resident write seam derives its sort and domain from the row (as `assert_checked_persistent` does) and refuses loud when it cannot, or the constants are a stated decision at their site naming why this seam is anthill-todo's alone; cargo-test green via rustland/scripts/test.sh.
