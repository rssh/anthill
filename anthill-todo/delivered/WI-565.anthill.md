## description

Improve the diagnostic for a bare (unqualified) call to a sort MEMBER operation from outside its defining sort. Today guarded(box(...), n) where guarded is a member of sort Box, written at namespace scope, reports the terse 'type mismatch in guarded.apply: expected known operation or arrow-typed variable, got unknown functor' (TypeError::UnknownApplyFunctor in typing.rs). The BEHAVIOR is correct member scoping — a member's bare name is in scope only WITHIN its defining sort (a sibling member CAN bare-call it; verified), and from outside it must be qualified Box.guarded(...) or dot-dispatched receiver.guarded(...) (both verified working). Only the message is poor: it should recognize the unresolved functor IS a known member of some sort and suggest the qualified/dot form, e.g. 'guarded is a member of sort Box; call it as Box.guarded(...) or via receiver.guarded(...)'. Surfaced during WI-557 (a malformed test wrote a bare member call); independent of the precondition/requires machinery (a member with no requires fails identically). Acceptance: a bare out-of-scope member-op call yields a diagnostic naming the owning sort and the qualified/dot remedy; existing UnknownApplyFunctor tests for genuinely-unknown names unchanged.
