## Description

Finiteness Phase D (proposal library/003, OPTIONAL cleanup): replace the separate finite combinator carriers (FiniteMappedStream/FiniteFilteredStream) with conditional-instance reuse of the single mapped/filtered carrier — 'a mapped stream is finite when its source is finite' via the witness-sort + requires pattern (EqList precedent).
