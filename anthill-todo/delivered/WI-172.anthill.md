## Description

scaland mirror of WI-030 — SLD O(n²) optimization: lazy substitution in resolve/SearchStream.scala. stepChoicePoint had been eagerly walking every remaining goal applying the new substitution after each match: SearchStream.scala:191 (after a ground-fact match) and :206 (after a rule-body match). Fix: drop the eager applySubst-each from both paths; walk goals(0) lazily through frame.subst at goal-selection time in stepInit; memoize the walked form back into goals(0) so choice-point retries don't re-walk; skip the structural walk when σ is empty. Reduces O(n²) over the goal list to O(n × goal_size). Also fixed latent shape mismatch in Builtins.executeExtractSort surfaced by lazy walking — emit canonical nullary Fn shape (kb.makeNameTermFromSym) instead of Term.Ref(functor); pre-fix the eager apply silently overwrote the inconsistency.
