## description

SCALAND: A RULE'S DOMAIN IS A SCOPE, SPELLED AS AN UNTYPED TERM. WI-976 typed scope-hood (`ScopeId`, minted from a symbol) through `SymbolTable`, `SymbolDef.Resolved`, `ScopeInclusion` and the whole loader spine — but stopped at the KB's write boundary. `assertRule`/`assertFact` still take `domain: TermId`, so every production caller converts on the spot: `Loader` calls `kb.scopeTerm(scope)` at SIX assert sites (fact, entity_of, rule, proof, provides-clause, provides-block fact). FOUR of the six compute that term for the domain slot ALONE — fact, rule, proof, provides-block fact — and lose the call along with it; at `entity_of` and at provides-clause the SAME term also fills a fact ARGUMENT, so those two calls SURVIVE the conversion: the slot changes, the call does not. Three facts say the slot should be a scope: rustland already stores it as a symbol (`kb/mod.rs`, `domain: Symbol`), so scaland's `TermId` is the divergence and not the parity; `byDomain`/`ruleDomain`/`factDomain` have ZERO callers anywhere in scaland, so nothing reads the term form; and the domain is only ever a `byDomain_` index key plus one equality test in `assertFact`'s dedup. WORK: `domain: ScopeId` on `assertRule`/`assertFact`/`RuleEntry`, and key `byDomain_` on the scope ITSELF (`HashMap[ScopeId, …]` — the shape `SymbolTable.scopes` already uses and rustland's `HashMap<Symbol, Vec<RuleId>>` mirrors), not on an unwrapped raw. Two efficiency findings fall out for free rather than needing a hoist: `loadRuleHeads` recomputes `scopeTerm` once per rule in a rule BLOCK, and `loadProvidesBlock`'s FactI arm recomputes it per item, both for a scope constant across the loop. DO NOT convert the TERM-LEVEL readers — each needs a term because a term is what goes in that position — and there are THREE of them, not the two first written here: `registerEntityOf`'s parent sort, the `entity_of` fact's embedded argument, and `provides_clause`'s `sort_ref` argument. Cost is concentrated in tests (~24 sites, mechanically `kb.makeNameTerm("test")` -> `ScopeId.of(kb.intern("test"))`). ACCEPTANCE (amended — the original was unreachable; the miscount behind it is recorded as feedback on WI-984, which inherits this ticket's reasoning): ZERO of `scopeTerm`'s callers is a domain slot, and the FOUR that remain are all term-level — `registerEntityOf`'s parent sort, the `entity_of` argument, `provides_clause`'s `sort_ref`, and `Builtins.executeScope`'s bound answer. Say at the site which test fails when the change is backed out. Related: WI-976, WI-984.
