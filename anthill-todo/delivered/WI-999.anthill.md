## description

Implement proposal 059 R4 clause 3: a declaration in a sort scope may not capture a short name that already resolves there unless the new declaration is a legitimate override or a distinguishable refinement.

THE HAZARD: a main-entry body calls a bare imported operation and answers one value; adding a same-named declaration in the shared sort scope silently repoints the unedited body. The same effect is measured for operations, consts, and nested type declarations, so the check covers every declaration category that can win lookup and applies to the whole sort scope, not only secondary entries.

EXCLUSIONS ARE SEMANTIC. A member operation is allowed when the declaring sort provides, transitively, the spec that owns the captured operation; use `sort_provides` and do not key on whether resolution arrived through requires or import. A requires-only operation is not an override, but WI-1048 established that a confidently distinguishable signature is a deliberate refinement. Reuse `typing::requires_shadow_is_confusable`: the FiniteCollection map and filter refinements must remain legal, while a confusable requires-only shadow remains subject to refusal. Binders and entity variants are outside the declaration categories admitted by R3.

IMPLEMENTATION: run after pass 2, when provider relations are known. Do not assume that the captured symbol has a source declaration or Operation kind; resolver builtins may have no declaration and operation tags may ride another kind. WI-995 is a prerequisite because imports must become file-local before capture is evaluated against the final resolution model.

MIGRATION: after WI-982 removed reflect.KB.nonvar and reflect.KB.ground, the corpus has zero no-override capture sites. The two requires-only FiniteCollection sites are valid refinements and must not be migrated or refused.

ACCEPTANCE: drive the operation, const, and nested-type capture controls so the base behavior is observed and the capturing row is refused with both sites named. Drive a provided-spec override and the two WI-1048 refinements as legal controls. Drive a confusable requires-only shadow as refused. Run rustland/scripts/test.sh.
