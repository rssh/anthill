## Description

Typer effect-tracking bug (graded-effect rows): an effect-set carried as a type/effect parameter is represented INCONSISTENTLY between row-literal position and forced/effects position, blocking graded-monad instance conformance. Surfaced building the stdlib graded DelayMonad/Delay (stdlib/anthill/prelude/delay.anthill, proposal 047). REPRO: Delay.delayFlatMap declared -> Delay[T=B, E={E1,E2}] has a body forcing two sub-computations; the declared {E1,E2} reads the row-vars as a MERGE OF OPEN TAILS (merge[open[tail=E1], open[tail=E2]]), but the body's forced captured effects read as PRESENT LABELS (merge[present[label=E1], present[label=E2], empty_row]); the two do not unify ('type mismatch in delayFlatMap.return: expected Delay[E={merge[open E2, open E1]}], got Delay[E=merge[present E1, present E2, empty]]'). Independent of 'sort E=?' vs 'effects E=?' on the carrier and of bare 'effects Eff' vs braced 'effects {Eff}' on force (all tried). pure/delay/force conform; only the {E1,E2} merge in flatMap fails. ROOT: a param standing for an effect SET is not treated as a row variable (open tail) in effects/@-position -- it becomes a single present label -- inconsistent with how {E1,E2} reads the same vars. Fix likely makes effects/@-position over a row-set-valued param produce an open tail, or makes subtype_effect_rows reconcile present-label vs open-tail for the same var. SECONDARY (related, worked around with let): effects of a NESTED ARGUMENT-position call are dropped -- delayForce(f(delayForce(m))) inferred {E2}, dropping E1 from the inner delayForce(m); 'let a = delayForce(m) ; delayForce(f(a))' correctly accumulates {E1,E2}. So a call's inferred effect omits its argument-evaluation effects (at least inside a lambda body) unless let-bound -- effect under-reporting (soundness-adjacent). ACCEPTANCE: a graded-monad flatMap body forcing two sub-computations conforms to its declared E={E1,E2} return; a nested effectful argument-call effect is reflected without a let; the stdlib Delay instance loads; full anthill-core green. Discovered 2026-06-19 (delay.anthill DelayMonad, proposal 047). Rust typer.
