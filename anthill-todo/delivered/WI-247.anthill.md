## description

Migrate consumers (typer, eval, req_insertion, codegens) from Handle/OccurrenceStore to value-typed NodeOccurrence. WI-242 delivered the substrate (kb/node_occurrence.rs: NodeOccurrence struct, NodeKind enum, Expr enum, MatchBranch) and wired the loader to populate kb.op_bodies: HashMap<Symbol, Rc<NodeOccurrence>> for every operation body. This WI completes the migration: (a) typer reads bodies from kb.op_bodies and walks Rc<NodeOccurrence> instead of TermId+OccurrenceId; classification moves to NodeOccurrence's RefCell; (b) eval frame stack stashes Rc<NodeOccurrence>; recurses on tree; resolve_handle/split_handle calls deleted; (c) cpp-gen, rust-gen, smt-gen body walks switch to NodeOccurrence; the 4 Handle-unwrap sites in cpp-gen (lib.rs:1572, 1871, 2584, 2604) and field_name_from_apply_arg's unwrap (post-WI-241) become direct &node.kind matches; (d) OpInfoRecord.body becomes Option<Rc<NodeOccurrence>>; body_occ field deleted; (e) reflection ops body_of/head_of/args_of produce Value::Node(Rc<NodeOccurrence>); reflect stdlib renamed sort Occurrence -> sort NodeOccurrence; (f) once all consumers migrated, delete OccurrenceStore, OccurrenceId, ExprOccurrenceId, HandleKind::Occurrence, Term::Const(Literal::Handle(Occurrence, _)) construction in load.rs, split_handle, resolve_handle. Acceptance: HandleKind::Occurrence no longer exists; no split_handle/resolve_handle calls in typer/eval/codegen; every Expr child slot is structurally a Rc<NodeOccurrence>; reflection-ops on bodies return Value::Node; cargo-test green. Reference: docs/design/occurrence-as-value-type.md sections 'Migration touch-points' and 'What stays as-is'.
