## Description

Nested implication (`-:`) inside `forall` in rule bodies. The hereditary-Harrop step-case form used by structural induction: (forall(?h, ?rest), ?P(?rest) -: ?P(cons(head: ?h, tail: ?rest))). Distinct from WI-027 (plain forall in rule bodies) — this adds the inner implication arrow that scopes the inductive hypothesis. Deliverables: tree-sitter grammar for `-:` inside forall body; parse IR node (NestedImpl{antecedents, consequent}); load.rs lowers it preserving De Bruijn scoping under the forall binder. Acceptance: hand-written rule with `(forall(?x), Q(?x) -: P(?x))` parses, loads, and round-trips. Prerequisite for WI-106 (kernel emits this form) and WI-108 (SLD resolves it).
