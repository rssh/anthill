## Description

BUG: a comparison guard makes a rule FLOUNDER in the (out,out) mode instead of letting the body's own generators bind first — while the logically identical not(eq(..)) enumerates correctly. REPRO (probed, fact num(v: 1..3)): (a) 'rule any_pair(?x,?y) :- num(v:?x), num(v:?y)' queried with both columns free yields 9 GROUND rows — enumeration works. (b) 'rule distinct_pair(?x,?y) :- num(v:?x), num(v:?y), neq(?x,?y)' queried with both columns free yields ONE row with UNBOUND vars — the guard collapsed the whole enumeration into a floundered residual (expected 6). (c) The SAME rule applied as distinct_pair(1) — first column bound at the CALL — yields the correct 2 rows, so the rule is fine in mode (in,out) and broken in (out,out). (d) Guard POSITION is irrelevant: moving neq before the generators flounders identically, so this is not delay-and-rotate ordering but a rule-level caller-var pre-check (the one wi483_rule_body_method_eval_test.rs:49-51 names: 'a ground query skips the caller-var delay pre-check, so the rule body actually runs'). (e) DECISIVE: 'rule distinct_naf(?x,?y) :- num(v:?x), num(v:?y), not(eq(?x,?y))' — the SAME logical content via NAF — yields the correct 6 GROUND rows. So not(eq(..)) works exactly where neq(..) flounders. Consistent with kernel-language.md:1535/:1548 ('= never binds ... Because = never binds, not(eq(...)) is always safe') and with cut_test.rs:236-245, whose NAF generate-and-test yields its 1 expected solution with the head var free. WHY IT MATTERS: generate-and-test is THE logic-programming idiom, and its natural spelling (a comparison guard after the generators) silently degrades to a floundered residual whose columns are unbound — which today materializes as a bogus Relation row (WI-737) rather than an error. It blocks map colouring, distinctness constraints, and the eight-queens column test (WI-736) in the mode that matters (query with the answer free), while the workaround — spelling every guard as not(eq(..)) — is undiscoverable and inconsistent. FIX DIRECTION: the pre-check delays a rule on the CALLER's unbound vars without accounting for the body's own generators binding them first; delay-and-rotate should suffice (delay neq, rotate, let num bind, retry) and demonstrably does not. Either make the pre-check account for body generators, or route the comparison builtin through the same path NAF takes. Settle WHY neq and not(eq(..)) diverge — one of the two is wrong, and the divergence itself is the bug. ACCEPTANCE: distinct_pair with both columns free yields the 6 ground rows, matching distinct_naf; distinct_pair(1) still yields 2; guards on genuinely unbindable vars still flounder honestly (they must not be forced); cut_test/wi525 NAF suites green; a regression test pins neq and not(eq(..)) agreeing; cargo-test green via scripts/test.sh.
