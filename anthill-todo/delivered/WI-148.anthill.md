## Description

Variable-scope alignment for structured-proof steps. Today each step rule in a structured proof body has its own de Bruijn variable scope (convert_proof_step calls reset_var_scope). Result: a step head like `lte(?d_next, ?delta)` gets ?d_next ↦ var_0, ?delta ↦ var_1 in the steps scope, while the parent rule has its own scope where ?d_next ↦ var_X. The steps cited-lift then quantifies var_0/var_1 universally over Real, producing a vacuous forall axiom (e.g. forall (var_0 var_1 Real) ⇒ <= var_1 var_0) which is logically false. Fix: thread the parent rules var_map into convert_proof_step instead of resetting; step heads/bodies share the parents synthetic var_<i> names. Surface area: parse/convert.rs convert_proof_step removes reset_var_scope; load.rs encode_proof_step renders heads/bodies under the parents var scope; the loaders structured-body load arm passes the parents var_map down. Acceptance: lf1 step_distance_lemma h_geometric and h_envelope lift to forall axioms whose variables actually correspond to the parents distance/velocity vars, so the conclude clause by z3(logic: LRA) closes the chain. Depends on WI-147 (abs interpretation) for the lf1 case.
