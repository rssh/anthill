## Description

A BODY READS ITS OP-SCOPED SLOT ONLY ON THE TIE ROUTE, so a call-site SELECTION still cannot reach one. WI-822 LEG 1 built the op-scoped dictionary channel — an operation's own `requires` names frame slots after its parent sort's (op_dict_entries), a call site fills them from the per-call substitution HONOURING `selected` (build_op_scoped_dicts passes it to build_dep_projection), and eval places them (push_op_scoped_slots). What it did NOT change is which channel a callee's BODY reads: op_scoped_defer_location is called from ONE place, check_apply_iter's DispatchOutcome::Ambiguous arm, so a body call defers to an op slot only where a dispatch TIES and neither a value nor a bracket can answer (a receiver-less `zero() -> T` over two CONCRETE providers). Every other op-scoped body call is served by VALUE-DIRECTED dispatch, which never sees `selections`. CONSEQUENCE, unchanged and MEASURED at 99: wi841_call_site_selection_test::an_op_scoped_selection_that_could_differ_is_refused_not_ignored — `probe[Monoid = AddM](2, 3)` where AddM and AnyM both answer is REFUSED (TypeError::UnthreadableSelection), because accepting it would compute AnyM's 99, the provider the author did not name. WI-841 chose the refusal as the honest option and its own doc PREDICTED WI-822 leg 1 would lift it; WI-822 corrected that claim rather than lifting it, and WI-841 is Delivered, so this gap now has no owner. THE HARD PART, measured during WI-822 and the reason this is its own ticket rather than an inline follow-up: widening the deferral to EVERY op-scoped call (the obvious placement) broke 30 tests across wi842/wi843/wi855/wi876/wi886/wi869 plus the eta route. The shapes that fail are exactly those with NO call site to supply the slot from — a host `interp.call` (seed_entry_requirements cannot seed an op slot: a stand-in is rooted at the PARENT SORT and mis-dispatches), an eta'd Value::OpRef (CallClass::EtaOpRef carries only the sort bundle), a dictionary-directed dispatch through start_apply_deferred — PLUS anthill.prelude.Ord.max -> PartialOrd.gte, whose `requires Ord[T]` can only be answered by the frame's OWN __req_self and has no chain slot to forward from (there is no Strategy 0 for `the dep IS the enclosing spec`). SO THIS TICKET MUST DECIDE A PLACEMENT, not add a call: which routes may read an op slot, and what each of the supply-less routes does instead. Candidate increments, each independently measurable: (a) a self-slot strategy in build_dep_projection (`the dep is the enclosing sort's own spec` => var_ref(__req_self)), which is what Ord.max needs and is the smallest of the four; (b) op-scoped dicts on CallClass::EtaOpRef and Value::OpRef, so an eta'd requires-carrying op carries its own slots as it already carries its sort bundle; (c) a host-boundary spelling for an op-scoped slot, or a stated refusal that seed_entry_requirements cannot have one; (d) only then, widening op_scoped_defer_location's caller set — and re-running the 30. ACCEPTANCE: an_op_scoped_selection_that_could_differ_is_refused_not_ignored FLIPS to Ok(5) (AddM's answer, not AnyM's 99) with its sole-provider sibling still green; wi842/wi843/wi855/wi876/wi886/wi869 and wi420_eta_concrete_member_evals stay green; the WI-817 551 relay chain stays 551; the placement decision written into docs/kernel-language.md §5.2 beside WI-822's paragraph and 058-implementation.md's deferred row; full workspace green via rustland/scripts/test.sh.
