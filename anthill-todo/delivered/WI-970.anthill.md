## description

SCALAND: SIX PARSER SPANS HAVE AN `end` THAT IS NOT THE TOKEN'S END — five stretch over trailing trivia, one is zero-width. FOUND while converting the span-capture idiom in WI-965 (2026-08-03), and deliberately LEFT ALONE there because WI-965 was a pure refactor and each of these is a behavioural change.

MEASURED, not inferred. `name`, `simpleName`, `importPath`, `distributiveProjectionSeg` and `proofStrategy` end on a plain `~ Index` rather than the `~~ Index` every other production uses. fastparse's `~` skips whitespace BEFORE its right operand, so the captured end sits past the construct, inside the trivia that follows it. Probe: `simpleName` over the label in `rule lbl   :   p(?x)` reports width 6 for a 3-character `lbl` — the three spaces before the `:` are inside the name's span. `Span`'s own doc already names this shape ('`end` IS AN UPPER BOUND … `~~ Index` is the spelling that avoids it, and is what the item productions use') — these five are the productions that do not. THREE of the five are multi-token anyway, so no combinator fits them regardless; `simpleName` is the one that WI-965's `located(ident)` would have absorbed exactly.

SEPARATELY, `operationTypeParam` builds `mkSpan(idx, idx)` — a ZERO-WIDTH span at the parameter's first character, not the identifier token. It is what the WI-850 type-param-default refusal points at (driven: `operation f[T   = S](x: S) -> S` reports at 3:17, exactly T's first character), so a caret renderer would have nothing to underline.

WHY NOTHING CAUGHT EITHER: no reader consumes `end` today — `Span.render` prints `formatStart` only. Measured control from WI-965: swap the two new span combinators' `~~` for a whitespace-skipping `~`, i.e. inflict this same defect on ~30 sites at once, and the whole 311-test suite stays GREEN. The one width assertion that exists (`DeclarationSpanTest`, the `F` of `sort Parameterized[A, F[T]]`) sits where no trivia follows the token, so it cannot see it either.

DECIDE, then do: is the widened end a defect to fix, or is `end` an upper bound BY DESIGN (as `Span`'s doc half-says) and these five are within spec? If a defect: the five take `~~ Index`, `simpleName` collapses into WI-965's `located(ident)`, and `operationTypeParam` takes `located` too. ACCEPTANCE: whichever way it goes, the answer becomes a TEST — a width assertion over a construct with trivia after it, which no test has today, so the next `~`-vs-`~~` slip fails something. Say which test fails when the change is backed out; today the honest answer is 'none', which is why this ticket exists.
