## Description

WI-702/054 effectful-rewrite gate has a hole for a METHOD-SYNTAX RHS. typing::check_simp_effectful_ops collects the operation functors a @[simp]/@[unfold] rule mentions via collect_op_functors_in_value over the head equation, and its comment justifies not collecting from a dot_apply position with 'a DotApply redex never fires under simp (WI-279 try_fire returns None)'. That justification is STALE: since WI-279 INC2 the typer's try_fire_dot_rule DOES fire sort-scoped dot rules, and since WI-902 it instantiates and macro-expands their RHS. So a rule whose RHS is written in method syntax -- rule dr: dot_apply(?e, m, ?x) = ?e.effectful_method(?x) @[simp] -- keeps its RHS as a dot_apply TERM, effectful_method is never collected, effect_row_blocking_equations is never consulted, and the rewrite splices an External/Modify call into every matching call site with no diagnostic. That is exactly the duplicate/reorder/drop hazard WI-702 exists to refuse. NOT introduced by WI-903 (which only made macro_expanded_rhs_head's typed-bounds condition exact) and NOT driven -- raised by /code-review on the WI-903 diff, code-read only.
