## description

Simp rewriter convergence PHASE 3: unite the TERM and OCCURRENCE carriers onto ONE iterative driver. After WI-641 the two OCCURRENCE rewriters share one iterative work-stack (simp_rewrite::rewrite, parameterized over the SimpFirer trait), but apply_eq_rules still has TWO drivers split by carrier: a Value::Node redex routes through the iterative simp_rewrite::rewrite, while a Value::Term redex takes a hand-written RECURSIVE walk (resolve.rs apply_eq_rules steps 1-2: recurse apply_eq_rules(child, fuel-1) per subterm, rebuild Term::Fn, then top-fire via fire_simp_equation, fixpoint-recurse). The iterative driver itself is hardcoded to Rc<NodeOccurrence> (SimpFirer::fire and rewrite's Visit/Build/reassemble/for_each_child/is_rewritable are all occurrence-specific).

TWO CONSEQUENCES this removes: (a) the term carrier is HOST-RECURSIVE, so it can overflow on a deeply-nested term redex (no iterative heap walk like the Node path's WI-641 fix); (b) the two carriers bound fuel DIFFERENTLY and necessarily so today -- the recursive term walk must spend fuel on DESCENT DEPTH (fuel-1 per level) to avoid overflow, so a term nested deeper than fuel stops rewriting, whereas the iterative Node driver descends the whole tree and spends fuel only on the fire->refire chain (documented at resolve.rs apply_eq_rules doc, WI-641). Uniting removes both: the term carrier becomes iterative (no overflow) and both carriers get fire-chain-only fuel (same firing DECISIONS, same depth reach).

APPROACH: generalize simp_rewrite::rewrite (and Visit/Build/visit_node/build_node) over a CARRIER abstraction -- child iteration, reassembly, and the leaf/descend test -- so both a term (TermId / Value::Term, via get_term children + alloc Term::Fn reassembly) and an occurrence (Rc<NodeOccurrence>, via for_each_child + simp_rewrite::reassemble) drive the SAME iterative loop; SimpFirer::fire becomes carrier-neutral (&Value or a TermView) firing via fire_simp_equation. Then apply_eq_rules's Value::Term branch routes through the unified driver too, deleting the recursive steps 1-2. Keep firing decisions identical (fire at every node incl. bare leaves -- the WI-641 fire/descend split -- and match_view_oneway is unchanged). NOTE the driver already fires carrier-neutrally at the leaf (fire_simp_equation handles term + Node), so only the DESCENT/REASSEMBLY needs the carrier abstraction.

BUNDLED (optional, same convergence story -- split out if they grow): (i) add a has_simp_equations(kb) gate so apply_eq_rules short-circuits the whole walk when the KB has no indexed [simp]/[unfold] equations (simp_rewrite::run already gates once; needs an entry/inner split so the recursion doesn't re-check per level), and consider caching the indexed simp-rule id list instead of the per-node double rules_by_functor(eq)+rules_by_functor(unify) alloc in fire_simp_equation (pre-existing, amplified by WI-641's per-node firing). (ii) merge the resolver's simp_requires_guard_holds(&Value, non-spec-op->false) and the typer's simp_fire_guard_holds(&NodeOccurrence, non-spec-op->true) into one carrier-neutral guard -- blocked today by the &mut kb (value_type_term) vs &kb (typer holds immutable) split AND the different non-spec-op defaults (typer fires monomorphic identities unconditionally; resolver only reaches its guard behind equation_is_requires_guarded); resolve the borrow split first.
