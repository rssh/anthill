## Description

A host cannot ask before a call whether a carrier answers a spec's dispatch: its provision written and its conditions, its own requires, its named slots
WHO NEEDS IT. anthill-traces (private) runs a model of a spec sort: a carrier that provides it, called as Spec.op[Spec = Carrier](…) through Interpreter::call_bracketed_assuming_requires (WI-20261126-7BJGQ). At startup it refuses, by name, a carrier the call cannot use, rather than fail at the first step of a run. Several of the reasons are the typer's to decide, and no public question asks it short of making the call.
WHAT HAPPENS. Measured 2026-11-27 on 2fe680bc5 with anthill-traces' test binary, on scratch specs of its own. Each carrier passes sort_provides(carrier, spec) and is refused at every bracketed call: (a) it provides the spec only through another spec (T2 provides Mid, Mid provides Keeps, Mid with no constructor): 'a selection of Keeps = T2 at pu.Keeps.keep must name a sort that declares provides pu.Keeps[…]'; (b) a conditional provision whose condition does not hold (provides Keeps :- Ord[T = Named], Named deriving Eq and not Ord): 'unresolved: anthill.prelude.Ord[T = pu.Named]'; (c) a requirement of its own that does not hold (sort M requires Ord[T = Named]): the same; (d) a type parameter something requires and the bracket leaves unfixed (sort Box[T] requires Eq[T] … provides Plain): 'unresolved: anthill.prelude.Eq[T = pv.Box.T]'. The held forms run: :- Eq[T = Named], requires Eq[T = Named], an unconditional clause beside an unheld conditional one, requires Marker (a refinement, no dictionary), and Box[T] where nothing is required of T.
WHAT WAS TRIED. typing::resolve (public), on a SortGoal built from each requirement's or condition's spec view (its named arguments the bindings, carrier None, left_open empty, an empty ResolutionScope), answered as the call did on the shapes above. A review in anthill-traces (2026-11-28, read from code) found where such a goal departs from the typer's own, each piece crate-private: (1) whether a provision has an unconditional clause is provides_unconditioned_clause (pub(crate)); comparing provides_clause_count with the condition groups is the defect WI-20260925-P5G39 fixed in alternative_condition_goals; (2) a requirement that leaves a parameter unwritten: requirement_goal sets left_open from unwritten_requires_slots and completes it with complete_open_slots_at_carrier (pub(super)); (3) a view that binds an operation (combine = add): only type-parameter keys are bindings (is_type_param_binding, pub(crate)); (4) a named slot (requires O: Ord[…]): the dictionary build withholds a default (rung_for_dep), where resolve consults one. Re-deriving them outside drifts.
WHAT IS ASKED. One public question that answers as a call's selection and dispatch at [Spec = Carrier] do, without an operation or arguments: is Carrier a provider a selection accepts, does a clause of its provision hold (its conditions), and does every requirement its frame owes resolve, each as the dictionary build resolves it, rung included? On failure, each failing goal as the typer writes it, with its hint. The shape is anthill's to choose: a KnowledgeBase question over (spec, carrier), the dictionary a call at [Spec = Carrier] would be handed or why it cannot be built, or a check-only entry beside Interpreter::call_bracketed. A carrier with type parameters is answered at the bare carrier, as the call is.
DONE WHEN. (1) Each of (a)–(d) is answered as failing, naming the goal the call's refusal names; each held form as holding. (2) The review's four: an unconditional clause beside two equal conditional clauses holds; a requirement leaving a parameter unwritten that a provision answers holds; a requirement binding an operation is answered as the call is; a named slot only a default would settle is answered as the call is. (3) Agreement measured: for each row, the bracketed call is refused exactly where the question fails. (4) The back-out measured, and said at the rows.
