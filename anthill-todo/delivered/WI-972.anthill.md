## description

SCALAND: the file's LAST top-level declaration spans to end-of-input, swallowing every trailing newline, blank line and comment after it.

A ninth site of the class WI-970 repaired, and the first one no reading of the productions could have found. WI-971's corpus span-tightness audit (ParseSpanCoverageTest, `looseEnds`) is what surfaced it, on its first run, and that audit EXCLUDES this one case today - the exclusion is documented at its site and points here.

MEASURED, with a probe over the delivered parser (probe deleted; reproduce with any 3-line fixture):
  `fact p(x: 1)`            -> 0..12  tight
  `fact p(x: 1)\n`          -> 0..13  spans the newline
  `fact p(x: 1)\n\n\n`      -> 0..15  spans both blank lines
  `fact p(x: 1)\n-- done\n` -> 0..21  SPANS THE COMMENT
  `fact p(x: 1)\nfact q(x: 2)` -> 0..12 tight; the second is tight too

NOT A WI-971 REGRESSION: `git checkout HEAD -- AnthillParser.scala` and the probe prints byte-identical numbers. Present before the conversion.

NOT A `~` vs `~~` DECISION ANYWHERE, which is why the WI-970/971 sweeps could not see it. Every capture is at the correct offset; only END OF INPUT behaves differently. The trigger is a production whose LAST element is an optional or a `rep` (`metaBlock.?`, `typeExpr`'s trailing `rep`): mid-file its whitespace skip resets over the trivia, at EOF it does not. A production ending in a fixed token is unaffected - `namespace d ... end\n` is tight, which is why most stdlib files pass and `primitives.anthill` (top-level braced `sort`s) is the one that fails.

WHY IT IS FILED RATHER THAN PATCHED: the fix is not local to a production. Trimming trailing whitespace in `mkSpan` is the obvious move and is INSUFFICIENT - it does not cover the trailing-comment case above, since a comment is trivia the skipper consumed but is not whitespace. A correct fix either (a) finds why fastparse's optional/rep tail reset differs at EOF and stops it, or (b) gives `mkSpan` a real trivia-aware backward scan (the `ws` skipper already knows the comment forms; running it backwards is not free). Decide between them with a measurement, not by taste.

ACCEPTANCE: the exclusion in `ParseSpanCoverageTest.looseEnds` is DELETED and both audit cases stay green; a case in SpanEndTest pins a declaration at end-of-input followed by a comment; scaland-sbt-test green.
