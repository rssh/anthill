## Description

Retire the op_bodies side-table (op-body cache): collapse the operation body INTO the OperationInfo fact (body-as-fact), so the typing pass reads the full op signature INCLUDING the body as facts rather than a Rust-side keyed map it mutates. TODAY: op_bodies is a HashMap<Symbol, body_node> keyed by op symbol, read via the operation_body builtin (WI-305) and MUTATED by the typer (set_op_body_node write-back during simp, WI-283) -- a modifying side-table during typing. MOTIVATION (proposal 022 / typing-as-transform): the eventually self-hosted typing pass should be a declarative transform reading Sigma (incl. the body) as facts and DERIVING facts, not the Rust typer mutating a side-table; the body is keyed data (op Symbol -> body) that should ALSO be a fact, like the rest of the now carrier-agnostic OperationInfo value fact (WI-348). BLOCKED ON WI-370: putting the body in the OperationInfo fact makes the discrim-tree insert walk the head full nested structure, descending into the body Expr whose control-flow forms (let/if/match) read Opaque in occ_head (no DiscrimKey) -- the Phase-A panic. The two naive ways out are wrong or heavy: attach-as-leaf merges distinct opaque values; structural keying builds a per-body trie MIRROR no query prunes on, dups occurrence_term in occ_head, and risks deep recursion (simp went iterative, WI-278). The clean end-state (body in fact PLUS discrim-level body-shape queries without a materialized mirror) needs WI-370 custom-unification/custom-search at a discrim node: delegate the body subterm matching to on-demand TermView unify instead of trie descent. DESIGN PRINCIPLE: a side index should itself be a relation / the discrim tree, never a bespoke Rust map -- the discrim tree already IS the Symbol->fact index via name: Ref(op). DECOUPLED FROM WI-348 (which delivered the carrier-agnostic substitution/answer layer; the op-body collapse is a separate everything-is-facts concern). ACCEPTANCE: op_bodies side-table gone; the body rides in the OperationInfo fact; operation_body reads it from the fact; cargo-test green.
