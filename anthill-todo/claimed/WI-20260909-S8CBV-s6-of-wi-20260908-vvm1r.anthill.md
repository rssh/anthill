## Description

S6 of WI-20260908-VVM1R (umbrella): ATTRIBUTION BY PROJECTION ROOT -- let the requirement channel name a path-dependent projection, and key which dictionary a require means on the projection ROOT rather than on the bound SORT. This REPLACES S4's (WI-20260909-96ZTM) sort-matching selector and lets its anchored gate be lifted; it is not built on top of them.

WHY THE SORT CANNOT BE THE KEY (user, 2026-09-09, and it is the whole ticket). Two head bindings of one sort are not one carrier: if Leaf itself has a parameter, x: Leaf and y: Leaf may be Leaf[A] and Leaf[B], so x.T and y.T differ. S4 keys attribution on the bound sort and therefore CANNOT separate them -- rule p(?x: Leaf, ?y: Leaf) is refused there, and S4's own note records that as not delivered. The identity that does separate them is the PROJECTION ROOT: different expressions (different = not delta-equal) have different roots; delta-equal expressions are compatible.

NOT typeof, and not a runtime operation. This is path-dependent typing, and the substrate is already designed AND implemented: docs/design/path-dependent-types.md sec 4 -- 'equality is definitional conversion' -- delta-ground both sides and, if both stay neutral, check sigma-equality; 'a neutral equals only an identical neutral' (kernel-language.md 4331). unify_types OWNS that comparison (delta at the sites, zeta in unify_types, per that doc's sec 4.1). THIS TICKET MUST DELEGATE TO IT AND NOT RE-IMPLEMENT IT: S4 hand-rolled a structural key for the same question and /code-review found it wrong twice (head-only + SHORT name made Box[E = Leaf] and Box[E = Other] compare EQUAL; and it never canonicalized, which views_structurally_equal's own doc tells callers to do first).

Consequences, each of which REMOVES something S4 installed rather than adding to it: the sort-matching selector goes (it is what mis-handles an APPLIED bracket by matching its head and dropping its arguments, and what accepts an INTRODUCER bound by symbol collision); the >1-anchor refusal narrows to the genuinely unattributed case; and the ANCHORED gate can be LIFTED, because a projection root attributes on the WITNESS path too -- match the named root against the witness call's carrier argument, which is the thing scan order currently decides.
